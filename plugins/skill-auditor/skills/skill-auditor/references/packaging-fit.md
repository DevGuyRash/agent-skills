# Packaging Fit

Use packaging fit when the capability's required property may be carried more reliably by something
other than its current skill or plugin shape.

Identify what the capability contributes that the base agent, user request, ambient context, host,
or existing tools do not. Judge the owner from that contribution and its hazards. Reusable judgment
may fit a skill; guaranteed event behavior may fit a hook; deterministic transformation or external
reach may fit a script or tool; a meaningful context or authority boundary may justify another agent.
These are examples, not a finite primitive taxonomy.

Do not recommend migration merely because another mechanism exists. A change in owner needs a
material reliability, authority, retrieval, context, reuse, or verification advantage. A hybrid is
useful only when each part has a distinct responsibility and observable boundary.

Inspect destructive, outward, privileged, or credential-touching effects against the authority the
host and maker actually grant. Preserve uncertainty when the relevant schema or runtime cannot be
verified. Do not manufacture an approval rule from general preference.

State the best-supported direction and why its owner carries the required property. If moving or
removing the capability would retire the standalone skill, make later skill-shape advice conditional
instead of polishing an artifact that should not survive. Name the evidence that could favor the
current package or reopen the direction.
