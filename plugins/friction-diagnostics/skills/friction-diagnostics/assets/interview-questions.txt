# Eliciting question pool. Format: field<TAB>question (one per line).
# Sampled at random by report-friction.sh --interview and by validation nudges.
# Rotation is deliberate: no single phrasing should become the template.
# Questions are domain-neutral: they must work for code, writing, research, ops.
actual_outcome	Paste the exact output, error, or sentence. Resist the urge to summarize.
actual_outcome	What is the verbatim evidence a stranger would need to see to judge this themselves?
actual_outcome	Which detail of the raw outcome is load-bearing: the exit code, the error class, the exact wording?
actual_outcome	If you can only keep one screenful of evidence, which lines survive?
expected_outcome	What did you predict, and which specific words or past experience grounded that prediction?
expected_outcome	How confident were you before acting, and what made it feel safe?
expected_outcome	Was the expectation written anywhere, or inferred? Quote it if written.
expected_outcome	What would you have bet on this outcome, and at what odds?
reading	What did you consult immediately before acting, and what did you take it to mean?
reading	Reconstruct the moment of choice: what options did you see, and why did this one win?
reading	Quote the exact words you acted on. What did you add to them that was not written?
reading	Where in the sequence did your model of the situation stop matching reality?
reading	What did you already believe before you looked at anything this time?
reading	Which signal did you dismiss or skim past that turned out to matter?
reading	What were you optimizing for in the moment - speed, safety, completeness - and how did that shape the action?
reading	If another agent had watched you work, at which step would they have objected?
decision	What did you actually do next? Past tense - the response you made, not the one you would make now.
decision	Which options were on the table in that moment, and what tipped you toward the one you took?
decision	Name an option you set aside. What made it feel worse than the path you chose?
decision	If you skipped or overrode something documented as required, what made that feel permitted right then?
decision	Quote the rule or instruction you weighed against, and state the reading of it that let your action through.
decision	Was there a moment you almost did something else? What was it, and what stopped you?
pivot_information	What fact, had it been on screen before you acted, would have stopped you?
pivot_information	Where does the missing fact actually live - a file, a doc, an output, nowhere?
pivot_information	Was the decisive information checkable in advance, and what would checking have cost?
pivot_information	If you could plant one sentence into your past context, what would it say?
pivot_information	Did anything you already had access to contain the answer? Why did it not surface?
pivot_information	Is the gap in the world (nothing states this) or in the reading (it was stated, you missed it)?
pivot_information	If this was caught before any harm, what fact should the next agent check first - and which source wins when they disagree?
sources	What did you trust that betrayed you: an artifact, an instruction, a tool, an assumption, a memory?
sources	For each trusted thing: what exactly did you believe it said or would do?
sources	State the belief as you held it before acting - with none of what you now know mixed in.
sources	Which trusted thing carries the wrong or missing sentence - and where exactly in it does the betrayal live?
sources	Was the betrayal in the text itself, or in what you added to the text?
recurrence_key	Name the trap so your future self recognizes it in two seconds.
recurrence_key	If this bites again next month, what 2-5 hyphenated words would you search for?
note	What is true about this incident that nothing above asked?
note	What would you tell a colleague about this that fits none of the fields?
