# Review Design

Use this reference when rubric content, grader choice, calibration, presentation topology, blinding,
or disagreement remains unresolved. Review interprets evidence; it does not manufacture a vote or
replace the execution population.

## Rubric as measurement model

Create a rubric for the user's actual decision. Honor maker criteria and governing authority. Where
they are incomplete, research applicable standards, established rubrics, domain sources, and current
evaluation practice broadly enough that an omitted authority is unlikely to change the decision.
Synthesize only what fits this objective; do not concatenate every category or copy a famous rubric
whose population and purpose differ.

Retain the smallest dimensions whose separate treatment can change the choice. A rubric is not a
reviewer response template. Avoid worksheets, fixed headings, word counts, universal verdicts,
arbitrary weights, totals, bands, or closed concern inventories unless the real interface or an
adopted contract gives them meaning. Preserve an unanticipated material concern even when it does not
fit a named dimension.

Freeze the rubric before candidate exposure. Its author must not see outputs, condition identities,
development expectations, or a desired winner. If later evidence requires a revision, keep it as a
new exploratory review. Every scale, weight, gate, or mandatory form needs a decision-grounded basis
and internal reachability; neat arithmetic is not evidence of meaningful measurement. Numeric
weights, thresholds, bands, or tie-break margins require maker-fixed losses, empirical calibration,
or a formal consequence model. Post-hoc prose about relative importance does not validate invented
numbers. A need to retain Notes or comparative reasons specifies information, not a worksheet: bind a
literal form only when a downstream interface needs it or behavioral evidence shows that it improves
review validity without displacing unanticipated judgment.

Validate that the rubric can distinguish a plausible consequential success, failure, and tradeoff,
cannot be won by mirroring its language, and does not grade a private expectation absent from the
worker contract. When it is intended to predict a downstream outcome, calibrate criterion validity
against applicable real observations, domain experts, trusted examples, or another supported proxy.
High judge agreement alone cannot establish that the criteria track the outcome.

Retain each consequential criterion's authority and decision force in the instrument. A house design
lens or evaluator preference may support a useful recommendation without becoming target truth,
portable conformance, or a hard eligibility rule. A diagnosis or repair named by the instrument
remains a proposition for reviewers to test unless the maker, target, adopted standard, or observed
outcome establishes it. Independent validation and blind agreement cannot repair this authority
error; both can reproduce it. Without a maker-set consequence model, a recommendation-level gain
does not compensate for a false claim, an omitted required property, or a stronger authority
violation.

Give every reviewer the same core decision meaning. A declared specialist lens can supplement it
without silently changing the comparison. A scale may be qualitative, ordinal, quantitative, or
mixed and must allow insufficient or not-assessable evidence. A trusted executable result need not be
re-scored by a model.

Every semantic grade or item carries reviewer-written Notes grounded in observed support,
counterevidence, and uncertainty. A number or label without those reasons is not a judgment. The bulk
of the review is a free-form comparative rationale explaining what materially served or failed the
user's objective, including interactions or concerns the rubric did not anticipate. Require
inspectable reasons, not private chain-of-thought or a prescribed reasoning ritual.

## Evidence suited to the property

Use executable checks for facts they can reliably decide and semantic reviewers for judgment. A
checker must reject a plausible wrong result and faithfully implement every source branch its claim
covers. Exhaustive execution of a partial model remains partial evidence.

An evidence manifest proves neither claim linkage nor semantic support. When durable traceability is
part of the handoff, a deterministic contract may require each consequential claim to reference a
declared, available artifact. That closes only the linkage boundary. Reviewers still determine
whether the referenced material is relevant, sufficient, non-circular, and consistent with contrary
evidence.

Every review layer must own a distinct unresolved property. A source-faithful deterministic result
does not become stronger because an executor reruns it and convenes an inner semantic panel before the
experiment's blind reviewers see the artifact. Executor-created reviewers are part of the measured
condition when the candidate actually includes that review system, or when they observe a different
population or residual semantic property. Otherwise keep the fact with the executable evidence and
leave comparative judgment to the experiment review. This separation prevents evaluation machinery
from becoming candidate content and being rewarded for its own volume.

A fresh reviewer can catch an omission in an artifact without creating new evidence about the
alternatives described by that artifact. Keep that artifact-QA contribution, any resulting rescue,
and its cost distinct from the option evidence and from the behavior of the unassisted condition.

Do not create a reviewer layer when adopted deterministic evidence has already fixed the factual
frontier and the only unresolved variable belongs to the decision owner. Review of an evaluator-made
profile, rubric, or summary is not a new observation of the alternatives. When its only material
finding repairs that intermediate, record self-generated process cost rather than task improvement.

Keep controller provenance and experiment machinery out of the comparison when they are not part of
the user's deliverable. Otherwise a condition can win by displaying more evaluation ceremony rather
than a better outcome. Supply trusted checks or provenance beside candidates only when the decision
depends on them and every candidate receives an equivalent opportunity.

A reviewer judges retained artifacts and outcomes; it does not become a representative user,
operator, player, deployed system, or business result by receiving a rubric. Preserve predictions
about those populations separately from observations of them.

Reviewer agreement also does not manufacture a preference owned by an absent decision-maker. When
the evidence supports a non-dominated frontier, reviewers can judge whether candidates expose and
reason from it faithfully; they cannot resolve the frontier by vote unless that reviewer population
is itself the authorized decision role under test.

## Reviewers and presentation

Use as many reviewers as consequence, uncertainty, and reviewer variability warrant. Repeating one
judge measures its stability; it does not create new task executions. Models from one family may
share blind spots or style preferences. Cross-family models, specialists, trusted mechanical checks,
and human calibration can reduce dependence when they fit the decision, but panel size cannot repair
an invalid task or rubric.

Honor user-selected reviewers, models, and efforts. Otherwise prefer capable review models and high
reasoning effort for nuanced judgment, subject to deployment fidelity, cost, latency, and
availability. Record requested and resolved host, model, version, effort, prompt, output, usage, and
timing. If a model or host is the condition under test, avoid making that identity part of the
reviewer's evidence.

A reviewer may compare two candidates, many candidates, one candidate against a contract, or bundles
of repeated runs. Pairwise views can simplify discrimination; listwise views can preserve tradeoffs.
Choose the view that answers the decision rather than generating every possible pair.

Randomize or counterbalance order independently enough to distinguish candidate identity from
position. In a multi-way view, superficial aggregate balance is insufficient when one candidate
never changes slot within the matched artifacts. Preserve the opaque shared case/block identity
needed for matched interpretation.

Blinding means the harness withholds a named identity; it does not prove that content, metadata,
style, or outside knowledge could not reveal it. Remove nonessential identity cues only when doing so
does not change the artifact being judged. State exactly what was concealed and retain remaining
inference risk. Condition assignment, authorship, candidate identity, and answer content are
different concealment claims.

## Judgment, reveal, and disagreement

Each reviewer commits a substantive judgment before mapping reveal. The record may identify a better
result, a tradeoff, or no distinguishable difference and must cite material evidence from the supplied
artifacts or trusted checks. Preserve Notes, any meaningful scores, free-form assessment, and
conclusion together.

After reveal, check whether preference follows the candidate rather than anonymous label, position,
formatting, or reviewer-family signature. Counterbalancing can expose a failed evaluator without
correcting it. Do not turn that failure into a tie-breaking vote.

Fact-check every decisive reviewer attribution against the candidate it describes and the public
contract. Reviewers can attach a real defect to the wrong anonymous result, reward a fabricated
finding, penalize warranted uncertainty, or import a private requirement. Preserve the committed
judgment as reviewer evidence but exclude unsupported premises from the condition conclusion.

Inspect disagreement instead of resolving it automatically by majority or a summed score. Reviewers
may expose different valid tradeoffs, order sensitivity, an invalid rubric, or a hard regression that
outweighs several preferences. Use a discriminating follow-up, qualified expert, trusted outcome,
user decision, or preserved uncertainty at the strength each supports.

## Evidence base

- [OpenAI evaluation best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices)
  supports task-specific criteria, calibration, and discriminative comparison.
- [Anthropic's agent-evaluation guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
  distinguishes outcome, transcript, code, model, and human graders.
- [GDPval](https://openai.com/index/gdpval/) uses blind domain-expert comparisons and treats its model
  grader as an experimental approximation rather than a replacement.
- [Judging the Judges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07791) measures position bias and repetition
  stability in pairwise and listwise model judgment.
- [Nine Judges, Two Effective Votes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29800v1) reports correlated judge
  errors that make nominal panel size overstate independent evidence.
- [PReMISE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30803v1) treats rubrics as measurement specifications and
  reports that agreement does not imply adversarial robustness.
- [RuVerBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29920v1) reports residual frontier-model noise in rubric
  verification and diminishing returns from majority voting.
- [Policy Invariance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06161v1) reports verdict changes under
  meaning-preserving policy rewrites.
- [Criterion Validity of LLM-as-Judge for Business Outcomes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00022)
  reports heterogeneous relationships between rubric dimensions and a verified downstream outcome.
