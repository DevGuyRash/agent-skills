# Validity and Attribution

Resolve each property through the closest rightful authority: source truth, executable state or behavior, direct downstream outcome, qualified expertise, intended-user behavior, rightful preference, or a justified composition. These are examples, not a resolver inventory. Prefer an acceptance relation over one answer when several outcomes can validly satisfy the governing contract.

Every source, checker, oracle, simulation, rubric, grader, reviewer, and interface is partial. Consistency, confidence, agreement, automation, or secrecy does not grant maker authority. When a plausible instrument defect could reverse the action, test it before candidate exposure with evidence capable of correcting it. Look for correlated self-confirmation when the observation generator and resolver share one unsupported premise.

Give reviewers the claim or property to resolve, native evidence, applicable authority, admissible sources, and consumer consequence. Preserve an open route for decisive evidence not anticipated by a rubric. Reviewer count, expertise, identity, assignment, replacement, and completion follow the live uncertainty and consequence. Use a capable reviewer for consequential interpretation unless reviewer fidelity or cost is under test, without hard-coding a model or effort.

After judgment and before synthesis, verify factual premises that control the action against the strongest retained source capable of correcting them. Preserve the original judgment and reconciliation separately. Agreement and arithmetic cannot repair a false premise, average away a veto, supply absent preference authority, or replace the execution population.

Keep mechanical termination, infrastructure behavior, task validity, candidate behavior, instrument validity, and causal attribution distinct whenever collapsing them could change the action. An unsatisfiable or authority-incoherent task does not establish candidate failure. Failure on a valid task under the frozen policy is candidate evidence even if another policy could rescue it. An infrastructure or capture failure follows the prospective policy and is not automatic permission to rerun.

Assign an underlying cause only when evidence distinguishes it from plausible alternatives and the attribution matters to the action. Strong repair or generalization claims require intervention, ablation, counterfactual contrast, or independent held-out confirmation capable of failing under the explanation. Seek the earliest actionable cause supported by evidence, not the deepest imaginable story.

Bound population, prevalence, causal, and deployment claims to what was observed. Preserve decision-changing uncertainty separately when its implications or remedy differ. When evidence cannot identify a winner, cause, authority, or valid comparison, state that limit rather than resolving it through confidence, majority vote, or presentation.
