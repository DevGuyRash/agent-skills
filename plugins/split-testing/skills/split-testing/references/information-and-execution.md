# Information and Execution

Comparability comes from a common governing relation, not necessarily identical prompts, resources, tools, or output forms. A controlled-component claim may require a common harness; an end-to-end choice may require each alternative's credible native scaffold. If both distinctions could change the action, obtain the least-cost contrast capable of revealing their interaction.

Reconstruct each condition's effective exposure when it matters: system, developer, ambient, and user instructions; attachments and source versions; tools, permissions, environment, model and effort; timing, retries, assistance, selection; and the consumer interface. Every behavior-changing difference must be the intended contrast, a declared native condition, an accounted nuisance, or an inference limit.

Treat authorship, instrument construction, execution, review, causal investigation, synthesis, and presentation as functions that may be combined or separated according to exposure risk, not as mandatory roles or stages. Use fresh contexts when prior exposure could alter the observation or judgment. Supply authoritative sources directly rather than lossy summaries, while keeping controller hypotheses, expected rankings, sibling outputs, private criteria, and preferred methods outside functions that would not receive them in intended use.

Do not impose semantic response templates, requested process narration, or explanatory artifacts solely for controller convenience. Preserve native outputs and raw responses. When transport requires extraction or normalization, keep the original payload and treat the transform as a separate instrument that must preserve materially different valid forms and hidden vetoes.

Choose host-native sessions, processes, workspaces, containers, users, or direct human exposures according to the property being observed. A process coordinator or separate directory does not by itself establish independence, read isolation, identity concealment, or freshness. Identify shared state and either account for it, alter the unit, or narrow the claim.

Record the runtime and intervention facts needed to identify the tested condition, including requested and resolved model or effort when material. If a model, scaffold, retry, assistance, or selection policy is itself under comparison, treat it as part of the condition. Simulated audiences and expert personas are diagnostic proxies unless validated as a consequence model for the intended population.

Use a disposable or faithfully simulated boundary for destructive, privileged, credential-touching, protected-state, or outward work. Do not cross a permission, safety, external-effect, or consequential-cost boundary merely because stronger evidence would benefit the comparison.
