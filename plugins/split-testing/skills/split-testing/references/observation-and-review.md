# Observation and Review

Use the nearest legitimate resolver for each consequential property. Depending on the claim, that may be an authoritative source, executable final state or behavior, direct downstream outcome, qualified expertise, intended-user behavior, rightful preference, or a composition. Prefer an acceptance relation over one reference answer when several outcomes can validly satisfy the governing contract. These are examples of authority relationships, not a resolver taxonomy.

Treat task authorship, instrument construction, execution, review, causal investigation, synthesis, and view authoring as optional functions, not mandatory roles or stages. Use fresh contexts at influence-sensitive semantic handoffs when prior exposure could change the result, while preserving continuity inside work that genuinely requires it. Combining functions is valid when the merged exposure cannot invalidate the inference; otherwise separate them, disclose dependence, or narrow the claim. Fresh context must not mean impoverished context: supply complete authoritative sources and legitimate inputs rather than lossy upstream summaries.

Observation authors normally receive the decision-bearing use, authority, population, horizon, authorized source universe, and effect boundaries—not candidate outputs, expected rankings, reviewer preferences, or controller-authored failure topology. Candidate-informed challenges begin a new prospective diagnostic round. Executors receive the actual task, legitimate inputs, authority, environment, tools, tested assistance and retry policy, boundaries, and real consumer interface—not comparative purpose, sibling outputs, hidden checks, preferred decomposition, or convenience templates.

Every source, checker, oracle, simulation, rubric, grader, reviewer, and interface is partial. Privacy, consistency, confidence, agreement, or automation does not grant maker authority. Validate a consequential instrument before candidate exposure when a plausible defect in it could reverse the action, hide a veto, or make the target property unobservable. Do not manufacture a separate validation artifact when correctness is already mechanically or authoritatively established at the relevant boundary.

Use evidence capable of correcting the instrument. Where applicable, challenge it with outcomes known from stronger authority to be valid, invalid, near the boundary, or shaped like a shortcut. Detect correlated self-confirmation when the observation generator, expected result, and resolver inherit the same unsupported premise. Agreement among components sharing that premise is not independent validation.

After blind judgment and before synthesis, reconcile each factual premise that controls the action against the strongest retained source capable of correcting it. Preserve the original judgment and the reconciliation separately: this check can disqualify a judgment's evidentiary use, but it cannot retroactively rewrite what the reviewer observed or supply missing maker authority.

For executable behavior, establish relevant solvability and reset behavior without inventing a universal oracle count. For semantic, professional, perceptual, or interactive work, calibrate through the experts, users, downstream effects, sources, or trusted examples the live decision authorizes. Judge agreement alone establishes only agreement.

Give each reviewer only the material and authority needed for the property they resolve. Reviewer identity, count, expertise, assignment, replacement, and completion policy should follow the uncertainty and consequence at hand, not a fixed panel shape. An added reviewer is warranted only when their observation can change the action, reveal a boundary, test instrument validity, or reduce uncertainty that matters.

Reviewers need an open route for decisive evidence that a predefined interface did not anticipate. Give synthesizers atomic evidence, provenance, tested policies, missingness, limits, closure conditions, and rightful values rather than upstream winner narratives when the underlying evidence is available. Keep identities blind until identity is intrinsic to the property or legitimately required. Prefer a capable reviewer for consequential interpretation unless reviewer deployment fidelity or cost is itself under comparison; no model or reasoning setting is a runtime constant.

Produce multiple task designs, instruments, reviews, or syntheses only when disagreement among them could change the action or expose instrument risk. Freeze any confirmatory selection or composition before candidate exposure. A vote is evidence about reviewer responses, not an automatic decision rule. Simulated audiences and expert personas are diagnostic proxies unless validated as a consequence model for the intended population; use credible intended-user exposure for preference-owned decisions when available and otherwise bound the conclusion to the proxy.

Keep factual resolution and rightful preference distinct. A reviewer can identify an unsupported claim or missed defect without acquiring authority to choose the maker's utility, taste, risk tolerance, or acceptance boundary. If the intended audience itself owns the preference under test, observe that audience under a credible exposure and bound the conclusion to that population and context.

Capture enough direct evidence to connect each consequential conclusion to the observation that bears on it. Preserve materially different uncertainty sources when their remedies or implications differ, but do not force them into a fixed category list merely for reporting symmetry.
