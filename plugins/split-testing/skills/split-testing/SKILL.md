# Split Testing

Determine the least-cost defensible basis for the live decision, or establish truthfully why no valid comparison is available, without making the user invent an evaluation method. Sufficiency means strong enough for the action and its consequences, not merely cheap or available. The decision governs the work; a polished test package, large panel, precise score, elaborate view, or familiar experimental shape is not success when it cannot change the action or support the claim.

Act autonomously on ordinary design. Establish the live action or comparative claim, rightful authority, intended population and horizon, consequential boundaries, alternatives, and the actual system policy under which they are being compared. Infer fit observations, conditions, resolvers, variation, and stopping from that context. Do not ask the user to choose routine metrics, cases, graders, repetition, topology, or presentation when defensible choices can be made. Ask only when an unresolved value, authority, permission, safety, external effect, or consequential budget could reverse or prohibit the action. Infer only consequence relations entailed by supplied goals and governing constraints. Do not infer taste, risk tolerance, rights, fairness weights, acceptable harm, or irreversible tradeoffs. Do not manufacture non-dominance from values absent from the supplied context; for a low-cost reversible choice within delegated authority, make a disclosed provisional choice and name a concrete reversal condition rather than asking a routine question.

Treat every instruction surface as part of the tested exposure. An influence is contaminating when it can change an observation or judgment but is not part of what the decision is comparing, the intended role or deployment, governing authority, a real consumer interface, or the smallest prospectively validated control for a demonstrated integrity hazard. Freeze the controller-level decision relation and tested policies, expose each role only to the legitimate inputs, authority, boundaries, and interface it needs, retain the exact effective input stack, and capture mechanical identity outside the substantive work whenever possible.

Reuse evidence that already resolves the live distinction. Otherwise arrange only observations capable of exposing a decision-changing difference, veto, genuine tradeoff, invalid inference, or actionable cause. Bind retries, replacement, assistance, resources, selection, and deployment policy into each tested condition. Preserve result-sensitive choices prospectively, keep representative evidence distinct from diagnostic challenge evidence, and do not collapse outcomes, conditions, populations, horizons, constraints, or uncertainty when that collapse could change the action or hide a real boundary. Bound what the evidence says without inventing what the action requires: a decisive veto can resolve the live action without proving a broader positive claim when the remaining alternatives already meet maker-set requirements.

Resolve each consequential claim through the closest legitimate evidence available and keep every instrument subordinate to rightful authority. When an adapted instrument or semantic review can determine the action, validate its authority boundary and ability to expose a plausible decisive failure before candidate exposure. After judgment, verify every decisive factual premise against trusted evidence before treating the judgment as decision evidence. Challenge other checkers, oracles, simulations, graders, or review interfaces only while a plausible decision-reversing instrument failure remains. Keep observed behavior separate from causal explanation; claim repair or generalization only to the strength supported by intervention or independent confirmation. Bound the conclusion to what was actually observed, and stop when the action is stable, a decisive requirement is settled, no authorized identifying observation exists, or more evidence costs more than its expected decision value.

Subagents are optional resources, not evidence primitives. A fresh process, session, workspace, host, or human exposure helps only when it supplies the boundary the inference needs. Shared state makes observations dependent unless a defensible reset, counterbalance, redesigned unit, or narrower claim addresses it.

Use no universal sample-size minimum or maximum. When variation matters, prospectively set an initial budget, replacement rule, and stopping rule from the live decision, plausible failure modes, and available resources. Do not cap alternatives or useful work merely to fit host concurrency; queue it while honoring actual authority, budget, safety, hardware, and external-effect boundaries, and stop when more evidence no longer has expected decision value.

When a caller supplies a versioned comparative-evidence request, preserve its exact frozen bytes and unknown fields, honor its authority and prohibited effects, and return evidence against its closure conditions without taking over the caller's decision. The caller retains authority over why the evidence was requested and what consequence follows.

## Completion

Return the least-complex adequate expression of what the evidence supports, its consequential limits, and what would reopen it. A supported direction, conditional statement, unresolved distinction, deterministic refutation, or refusal to claim a valid comparison may each be the honest result; do not force the result into a fixed vocabulary. A conversational answer is sufficient unless a persistent consumer interface is actually delivered. Verify files, links, packages, executables, or other durable boundaries in the environment where the consumer will use them.
